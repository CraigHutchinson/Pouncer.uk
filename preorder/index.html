Before you sign up, read the honest bit

Pouncer detects a pounce and sounds a cue. Whether that reduces what a cat catches is being measured, and is not yet proven — the closest device ever tested, a bell, showed no effect at all. We publish that on our own front page, and we will publish our trial result whichever way it falls.

If you want something that works today, change your cat's food and play with them for ten minutes a day. Both beat every collar ever tested. Pouncer is designed to sit alongside them.
