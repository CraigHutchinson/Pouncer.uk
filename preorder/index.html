What remains to be tested

Pouncer is being developed to recognise pre-pounce movement and give a brief warning during the stalk. Its effect on wildlife has not yet been demonstrated. Results for existing bell collars vary across studies, and we do not claim that Pouncer outperforms them.

Diet and daily play have supporting evidence from a controlled trial. Pouncer is intended to complement care for your cat, not replace it. We will publish our trial results whatever they show.
